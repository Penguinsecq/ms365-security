# SC-300 | Microsoft Certified: Identity and Access Administrator Associate

I just recently passed the SC-300 Microsoft Certified: Identity and Access Administrator Associate exam, so I'd like to share how did I prepare to pass SC-300 exam.

## Why SC-300
In cyber security world nowadays, one of the most important thing is "Identity". Identity is the new security perimeter in modern security foundation such as zero trust. Especially in cloud computing, remote work, and also hybrid environments because attackers now focus on stealing digital identities and credentials. They don't have to break through physical network perimeters or defenses like firewall, IPS & IDS or endpoint protection. Without proper and strong identity management, the more identities you have, the larger your attack surface becomes.

Personally, I have 3.5 years experience working in MS365 admin center, Entra ID, Defender, Exchange, Intune, Purview, etc. I always have to create, make changes, delete, assign user accounts, groups, conditional access policy, Enterprise application, etc. I have passed and earned Microsoft Applied Skills in Identities and access using Microsoft Entra before.

## Exam details (please check the official exam page for current details at the time you take the exam)
**Price**: 165 USD in the USA at the time I checked, pricing varies by country or region. I'm not in USA and also, I luckily received free exam voucher from Microsoft AI Skills fest event.
**Duration**: 100 minutes at the time I checked, excluding any additional check-in or admin procedures.
**Passing score**: 700 out of 1000.

## My Preparation
1. Official study guide: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/sc-300
2. Official Microsoft Learn SC-300: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/identity-and-access-administrator
3. Lab tenant. I spin up a trial tenant with Entra ID P2 and Entra Suite for free. Just follow the official MS learning, it will guide you how to get the free trial.
4. Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. I used AI to help me summarise and explain more detail for any topics that I could not understand. They also can help generating questions for practicing.
5. Microsoft SC-300 course on Youtube: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLahhVEj9XNTf6lWUbZLBNULQ7uVqM5Sad

## Exam Day
As this was the first time taking exam in overseas, I arrived exam center 30 minutes before the scheduled start time, I wanted to give myself enough time to prepare for everything such as wearing heat-tech, went to toilet and drinking a glass of water, because at my test center, you can only bring your id card, no mobile phone, or debit card. 

During the exam, I initially felt nervous as I encountered some long questions. I tried to read all information and answer them carefully. After spending first 30 minutes, I found that I had to be faster, so,  I skipped all the long questions, marked them to be reviewed later. Once, I got all short questions done, I came back and did the long ones. **My biggest takeaway is that time management is very important in this exam**. I completed all question in 99 minutes. One thing that you should know is, at the time I took exam, you can utilise MS Learn, it was very helpful and I use it for a few questions, however there is limitation, you can't search anything like you did at home. I would suggest to dedicate some time to see how to search and what is the result coming up before taking the exam. However, I would not suggest you to use it for all questions, as said, be careful with time management.

Overall, I found that practical experience was valuable. Be familiar with the MS365 interface and effective time management can also help you feel more confident on exam day.

I hope my experience helps anyone preparing for the SC-300 exam. Good luck with your studies!
